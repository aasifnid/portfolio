Decision-alts treatment — 5 directions

Each option shows the same content (Decision 1 — Shape) embedded in the same prose context (body paragraph before, image caption after) so you can judge how well the treatment connects to the surrounding flow.

Option 1

Inline prose — fold alternatives into the narrative

Zero chrome. The exploration is described in flowing prose. Reader gets the rigor as part of the storytelling. Most narrative, least table-like. Pairs naturally with surrounding body text.

The spec called for a "four-quadrant wheel" but didn't define the visual structure. I explored two tracks: the shape itself, then the visual treatment within the picked shape.

I tested three shapes for the wheel. A grid menu made the framework less visible. A modal sequence — one screen at a time — broke the feeling of narrowing in on the emotion. I landed on a circular wheel: the framework itself became the surface students tapped on.
