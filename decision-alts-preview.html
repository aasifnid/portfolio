Decision-alts treatment — 5 directions

Each option uses real Decision 1 (Shape) content from the Feel Check case study — same body prose before, same ideation image and caption after. Judge each treatment by how naturally it connects to the surrounding flow.

Director's recommendation

Option 2 — Annotated thumbnails

It's the only treatment that shows the ideation instead of describing it. Thumbnails per option visualize the actual exploration; the picked one carries an accent border + checkmark; rejected ones sit quietly beside it. Reads as a designer's exploration board, not a data table. It also replaces the standalone ideation image below (the row of thumbnails IS the image) — eliminating one of the two "abrupt table" interruptions in the current flow. Pre-req: 3 small sketch thumbnails per alts block (placeholder shown here, real assets to create later).

Option 1

Inline prose — fold into the narrative

Zero chrome. Alternatives are described in flowing prose. Reader gets the rigor as part of the storytelling. Most narrative, least table-like.

The spec called for a "four-quadrant wheel" but didn't define the visual structure. I explored on two tracks: shape, then treatment within the picked shape.

I tested three shapes for the wheel. A grid menu made the framework less visible. A modal sequence — one screen at a time — broke the feeling of narrowing in on the emotion. I landed on a circular wheel: the framework itself became the surface students tapped on.

Early ideation — bringing the Wheel of Awareness to a screen, not a diagram.
